Feedback and overlays

Everything that interrupts, confirms or reassures. Alerts stay, toasts pass, dialogs block. Choosing between them is the whole discipline.

Alerts and banners

Inline messages that stay until the situation changes. Four tones, and the tone is chosen by consequence, not by mood.
